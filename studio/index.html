Make it yours

Draw, add a photo, or add words. Choose colors and a brush on the left.

Layers are see-through sheets. Draw on one without changing the others. The top sheet appears in front. Select a sheet to edit it; Hide and Undo let you experiment.

Choose a car part above the canvas. It turns upright and zooms in automatically. Gray gaps are not part of the car.

Turn the paper, not your head. Turn left and Turn right rotate the canvas. Tilt lets you choose any angle. “See all” resets the view. None of these change your exported artwork. Scroll to zoom.

Save keeps editable layers. Download wrap makes the PNG for a parent to upload to Tesla. It changes the car on the screen, not the real paint.

B: draw · E: erase · I: pick color · Space + drag: move flat view · [ / ]: brush size · ⌘/Ctrl Z: undo. Photos stay on this device. Test touch and stylus behavior on your device.
